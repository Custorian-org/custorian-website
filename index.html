137 controls.

Each control is an auditable, measurable requirement that a digital service must implement to protect children. 128 are required for conformity. 9 are addressable. Full register: Annex A. Read on Zenodo.
CS-AC · Age Controls (23)
CS-AC.1.1 — Platform SHALL implement age verification for all users
CS-AC.1.2 — Age verification SHALL use at least two independent signals
CS-AC.1.3 — Self-declared age SHALL NOT be sole verification method
CS-AC.1.4 — Platform SHOULD support eIDAS digital age credentials when available
CS-AC.1.5 — Age verification SHALL NOT collect more data than necessary
CS-AC.2.1 — When age cannot be verified, system SHALL default to 13–15 bracket
CS-AC.2.2 — If primary verification fails, system SHALL fall back to secondary signals
CS-AC.2.3 — All age verification events SHALL be logged for audit
CS-AC.3.1 — Settings SHALL be maximally restrictive by default for child accounts
CS-AC.3.2 — Default settings SHALL differ by age bracket (8-10, 11-12, 13-15, 16-17)
CS-AC.3.3 — Location sharing SHALL be off by default for all minors
CS-AC.4.1 — Age-restricted features SHALL be actually gated, not just labelled
CS-AC.4.2 — Direct messaging from strangers SHALL be disabled by default for under-16
CS-AC.4.3 — Public profile visibility SHALL be off by default for under-16
CS-AC.4.4 — In-app purchases SHALL require parental approval for under-16
CS-AC.4.5 — Live streaming SHALL be disabled by default for under-16
CS-AC.5.1 — Platform SHOULD periodically re-verify age for accounts approaching thresholds
CS-AC.5.2 — Users SHALL NOT be able to change their age without re-verification
CS-AC.6.1 — Age verification methods SHALL be documented and publicly available
CS-AC.6.2 — Accuracy rates of age verification SHOULD be published
CS-AC.6.3 — Age verification SHALL NOT discriminate based on disability, ethnicity, or socioeconomic status
CS-AC.7.1 — Verifiable parental consent SHALL be obtained before processing data of children under consent age
CS-AC.7.2 — Parental consent mechanism SHALL be robust against child impersonation
CS-CD · Content and Design Safety (32)
CS-CD.1.1 — Platform SHALL detect and action CSAM using hash-matching technology
CS-CD.1.2 — Platform SHALL detect grooming patterns in communications
CS-CD.1.3 — Platform SHALL detect bullying and harassment patterns
CS-CD.1.4 — Platform SHALL detect self-harm and suicidal content
CS-CD.1.5 — Platform SHALL detect violent threat content
CS-CD.1.6 — Platform SHALL detect financial sextortion patterns
CS-CD.1.7 — Platform SHOULD detect AI-generated CSAM
CS-CD.1.8 — Detection SHALL be effective across all supported languages
CS-CD.2.1 — Platform SHALL NOT use infinite scroll for child accounts
CS-CD.2.2 — Platform SHALL NOT use notification pressure tactics (FOMO, streaks, urgency)
CS-CD.2.3 — Loot boxes and randomised rewards SHALL be clearly labelled with odds
CS-CD.2.4 — Platform SHALL NOT use deceptive design to trick children into sharing data
CS-CD.2.5 — Disabling safety features SHALL require more effort than enabling them
CS-CD.2.6 — Autoplay SHALL be off by default for child accounts
CS-CD.3.1 — AI may classify content but SHALL NOT generate child-facing intervention text
CS-CD.3.2 — Intervention language SHALL be age-appropriate for child's verified age bracket
CS-CD.3.3 — Interventions SHALL be culturally reviewed for each supported language/region
CS-CD.3.4 — No dialogue loop — system SHALL NOT sustain conversation with children
CS-CD.3.5 — Intervention content SHALL be version-controlled and publicly auditable
CS-CD.4.1 — Child-facing interventions SHALL be compatible with screen readers
CS-CD.4.2 — Intervention text SHALL meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and readability
CS-CD.4.3 — Crisis helpline actions SHALL be accessible to children using assistive input
CS-CD.5.1 — User reports SHALL be acknowledged within 24 hours
CS-CD.5.2 — Illegal content SHALL be actioned within 24 hours of reporting
CS-CD.5.3 — Content moderation policies SHALL be published and child-comprehensible
CS-CD.5.4 — Appeals process SHALL be available and accessible to children
CS-CD.5.5 — Age classification appeal mechanism SHALL be available to users
CS-CD.6.1 — Recommendation algorithm SHALL NOT amplify harmful content to child accounts
CS-CD.6.2 — Children SHALL be able to opt out of personalised recommendations
CS-CD.6.3 — Non-personalised content feed SHOULD be available as default option
CS-CD.7.1 — Targeted advertising SHALL NOT be served to children under 18
CS-CD.7.2 — Ads served to children SHALL be clearly labelled as advertising
CS-DM · Data and Privacy for Minors (18)
CS-DM.1.1 — Threat detection SHALL process data on-device by default
CS-DM.1.2 — Platform SHALL only collect data necessary for core functionality
CS-DM.1.3 — Data collection scope SHALL be documented and transparent
CS-DM.2.1 — All embedded SDKs SHALL be documented with data practices
CS-DM.2.2 — Analytics/tracking SDKs SHALL NOT profile children
CS-DM.2.3 — Third-party DPAs SHALL cover children's data
CS-DM.3.1 — Consent SHALL be informed, granular, and age-appropriate
CS-DM.3.2 — Consent withdrawal SHALL be as easy as giving consent
CS-DM.3.3 — Pre-ticked consent boxes SHALL NOT be used
CS-DM.4.1 — DPIA SHALL be completed for child data processing
CS-DM.4.1a — CRIA SHALL be conducted for any digital service likely to be accessed by children
CS-DM.4.2 — Children's data SHALL have defined retention periods and automatic deletion
CS-DM.4.3 — Children (or parents) SHALL be able to export their data
CS-DM.4.4 — Children (or parents) SHALL be able to delete their data and account
CS-DM.5.1 — Children's data SHALL be stored in compliance with data residency requirements
CS-DM.5.2 — Children's data SHALL be encrypted in transit and at rest
CS-DM.6.1 — Automated profiling of children SHALL NOT be used beyond core safety
CS-DM.6.2 — Precise geolocation SHALL NOT be collected from children unless essential and consented
CS-PR · Parental Rights and Controls (17)
CS-PR.1.1 — Platform SHALL provide a parental control dashboard
CS-PR.1.2 — Dashboard SHALL show safety alerts, not message content
CS-PR.1.3 — Dashboard SHALL be accessible on parent's device
CS-PR.2.1 — Parents SHALL NEVER see message content — alerts contain category, severity, timestamp only
CS-PR.2.2 — Parents SHALL NOT be able to enable keylogging
CS-PR.2.3 — Alert metadata SHALL NOT allow reconstruction of message content
CS-PR.3.1 — Children aged 13+ SHALL be notified that monitoring is active — non-configurable
CS-PR.3.2 — Notification SHALL explain what is monitored and why in age-appropriate language
CS-PR.4.1 — Detection sensitivity SHALL decrease and child agency increase with age
CS-PR.4.2 — 16+ teens SHALL be able to modify their own privacy settings
CS-PR.4.3 — Parental override SHALL decrease with child's age
CS-PR.5.1 — Child self-reporting mechanism SHALL route to counsellor or helpline, bypassing parent
CS-PR.5.2 — Crisis helpline SHALL be accessible with one tap from any safety alert
CS-PR.6.1 — Monitoring SHALL automatically cease when child turns 18
CS-PR.6.2 — Parent access SHALL be revoked at 18 — not parent-initiated
CS-PR.6.3 — On-device data SHALL be deleted within 24 hours of age-out
CS-PR.6.4 — Young adult SHALL have data access rights before erasure
CS-MR · Monitoring, Detection and Response (30)
CS-MR.1.1 — Platform SHALL implement real-time threat detection across 7 categories
CS-MR.1.2 — Detection SHALL cover: grooming, bullying, self-harm, violence, content wellness, sextortion, dangerous purchases
CS-MR.1.3 — Detection accuracy metrics SHALL be published per language, updated quarterly
CS-MR.1.4 — Detection SHALL NOT degrade performance by more than 10%
CS-MR.2.1 — CSAM SHALL be reported to competent designated authority within 1 hour of confirmed detection
CS-MR.2.2 — CSAM evidence SHALL be preserved per legal requirements
CS-MR.2.3 — Reporting channel to NCMEC/national authority SHALL be configured and tested
CS-MR.2.4 — Metadata preservation for CSAM and illegal content involving children
CS-MR.2.5 — Cryptographic integrity measures for preserved evidence
CS-MR.3.1 — Detection SHALL be consistent across all devices
CS-MR.3.2 — Institutional consent applies per child, not per device
CS-MR.3.3 — Detection history synced via encrypted on-device sync
CS-MR.4.1 — Incident response plan SHALL exist for child safety events
CS-MR.4.2 — Critical child safety incidents SHALL be triaged within 1 hour
CS-MR.4.3 — Incident response SHALL include notification to affected child and parent, except where it would compromise law enforcement investigation
CS-MR.5.1 — Evidence of child safety incidents SHALL be preserved per applicable law
CS-MR.5.2 — Evidence chain of custody SHALL be documented
CS-MR.6.1 — Platform SHOULD participate in industry threat intelligence sharing
CS-MR.6.2 — Detection patterns SHALL be updated at least monthly
CS-MR.7.1 — Platform SHALL publish transparency reports on child safety at least annually
CS-MR.7.2 — Reports SHALL include: detection volumes, categories, response times, appeals, removals
CS-MR.8.1 — Platform SHALL conduct systemic risk assessment for minors at least annually
CS-MR.8.2 — Risk assessment SHALL cover algorithmic amplification
CS-MR.8.3 — Risk mitigation measures SHALL be documented and reviewed annually
CS-MR.9.1 — Platform SHALL have responsible disclosure policy for child safety vulnerabilities
CS-MR.9.2 — Disclosed vulnerabilities SHALL be triaged within 48 hours
CS-MR.10.1 — Detection pattern updates SHALL be reviewed before deployment
CS-MR.10.2 — Pattern updates SHALL NOT introduce bias or suppress legitimate expression
CS-MR.10.3 — Rollback capability SHALL exist for false positive spikes
CS-MR.11.1 — Detection SHOULD account for atypical communication patterns
CS-GO · Governance and Policy (17)
CS-GO.1.1 — Platform SHALL designate a named Child Safety Officer (CSO)
CS-GO.1.2 — CSO SHALL report directly to executive leadership
CS-GO.2.1 — All staff with child data access SHALL complete annual child safety training
CS-GO.2.2 — Content moderators SHALL receive specialised trauma-informed training
CS-GO.2.3 — Moderator wellbeing programme SHALL be in place
CS-GO.3.1 — Third-party vendors processing child data SHALL be audited
CS-GO.3.2 — Vendor contracts SHALL include child data protection clauses
CS-GO.4.1 — Platform SHALL maintain documented regulatory compliance mapping
CS-GO.4.2 — Compliance mapping SHALL be updated within 90 days of new requirements
CS-GO.5.1 — Platform SHALL comply with child safety laws in every jurisdiction served
CS-GO.5.2 — Age of consent thresholds SHALL be configurable per jurisdiction
CS-GO.6.1 — Child safety governance SHOULD include external advisory input
CS-GO.6.2 — Child safety policies SHALL be reviewed at least annually
CS-GO.7.1 — Children's voices SHALL be included in safety design and review
CS-GO.7.2 — Feedback mechanisms SHALL be available and accessible to child users
CS-GO.8.1 — Organisation SHALL provide age-appropriate digital literacy resources to child users
CS-GO.8.2 — Safety features SHOULD build children's resilience and digital skills

6 domains.

The standard organises 137 controls into six functional domains. Each domain addresses a distinct dimension of child digital safety.
CS-AC · Age controls
Age verification, age estimation, age-appropriate access. 23 controls.
CS-CD · Content and design safety
Algorithmic safeguards, dark patterns, recommender systems, content moderation. 32 controls.
CS-DM · Data and privacy for minors
Children's data minimisation, retention, encryption, profiling restrictions. 18 controls.
CS-PR · Parental rights and controls
Parental dashboards, monitoring boundaries, age-graduated child autonomy. 17 controls.
CS-MR · Monitoring, detection and response
Threat detection across seven categories, CSAM reporting, incident response. 30 controls.
CS-GO · Governance and policy
Child Safety Officer, staff training, vendor management, regulatory mapping. 17 controls.
