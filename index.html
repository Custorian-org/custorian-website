April 3, 2026
The EU's legal basis for detecting child sexual abuse material expired.
No replacement is in force.
Regulation (EU) 2021/1232 allowed platforms to voluntarily scan private messages for CSAM. On March 11, Parliament voted 458–103 to extend until August 2027, but with strict conditions: targeted, proportional, no breaking encryption. The Council could not accept those conditions. Negotiations failed and the derogation expired on April 3. The replacement legislation, the EU CSA Regulation, remains in trilogue. 36.2 million reports of suspected child sexual exploitation were filed with NCMEC in 2023. The pipeline that generated them is now legally suspended in Europe.
European Parliament Digital Watch EDPS Opinion The Record State of Surveillance

The Custorian Standard recommends privacy-preserving architectures for child safety. The framework prioritises approaches where analysis runs locally, minimising the collection and transmission of children's communications data. This architecture is distinct from the server-side scanning contemplated by the expired derogation. Whether specific implementations fall outside ePrivacy scope is a question for legislators, courts, and data protection authorities. Custorian is seeking formal legal assessment.

Custorian Controls Framework

A comprehensive framework for child digital safety.

The Custorian Controls Framework (CCF) provides auditable controls across the full spectrum of child safety requirements. The standard is in formal development with Dansk Standard through the CEN/CENELEC process.

Age Assurance

Privacy-preserving age verification using multiple independent signals. Fail-safe defaults that protect children when verification is uncertain. Aligned with EU Digital Identity Wallet (eIDAS 2.0).

Ofcom 2024: 1 in 3 children aged 8-11 have a social media profile despite minimum age of 13. Source

Content & Design Safety

Harmful content detection, dark pattern prohibition, intervention design standards, and elevated moderation where minors are present.

EU Kids Online 2024: 45% of 12-16 year olds encountered harmful content in the past month. Source

Data Privacy

Data minimisation, privacy-preserving processing, children's rights impact assessments, and prohibition on using children's data for advertising or profiling.

ICSI 2023: 72% of apps in the Play Store children's category transmitted data to third-party trackers. Source

Parental Oversight & Child Agency

Balancing parental protection with children's evolving right to privacy. Safety alerts without content surveillance. Age-graduated autonomy aligned with UNCRC Article 5.

Pew Research 2024: Technology is the #1 reason parents say parenting is harder today. Source

Threat Detection & Response

Real-time detection across seven threat categories, incident response requirements, accuracy transparency, and mandatory reporting for CSAM and imminent danger.

NCMEC 2023: 36.2M reports of suspected child sexual exploitation. Source

Governance & Accountability

Child Safety Officer requirements, staff training, vendor management, regulatory compliance mapping, children's participation in safety design, and digital literacy.

No shared audit framework for child safety exists today. PCI DSS created this for payments. Custorian creates it for children.

The full standard is in formal development. Custorian is working with Dansk Standard on a CEN/CENELEC standardisation pathway. The standard has been cross-mapped against the DSA, GDPR, AI Act, UK Age Appropriate Design Code, UK Online Safety Act, COPPA, and the UNCRC. The white paper and regulatory cross-map are available on request.

For Platforms

Implement the standard. Achieve certification.

Platforms implementing Custorian gain a documented compliance pathway for DSA Articles 28, 34, 35, and 37 (minor protection, risk assessment, mitigation, and independent audit), and the EU AI Act. Comply once, satisfy many.

Custorian is a standard, not a product. Existing child safety tools are valuable; Custorian gives them a shared compliance framework to certify against. Standards don't replace products. They elevate entire industries. Like PCI DSS elevated payments. Like EPEAT elevated sustainable electronics. Custorian elevates child safety.

Compliance Model

Tiered by scale. Validated by evidence.

Governance

Guided by independent experts.

The Custorian Standard is governed by a non-profit and guided by an Advisory Council of independent experts in child safety, digital rights, EU policy, and child development. The Advisory Council reviews detection categories, intervention content, and standard updates.

The Advisory Council is forming. We are seeking expertise in child exploitation prevention, digital rights and privacy, EU regulatory policy, child psychology, survivor advocacy, and platform trust & safety.
