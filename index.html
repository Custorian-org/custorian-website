Custorian API Specification (CAS)

One API. 13 integrations. Every safety tool interoperates.

OpenAPI 3.1 specification backed by Microsoft PhotoDNA, Hive Moderation, Perspective API, Google Cloud Vision, Web Risk, Anthropic Claude, and Groq. Platforms implement it. Institutions consume it. Auditors verify it.

POST /custorian/v1/detect
Multi-layer threat analysis. Text routed through Perspective API (toxicity) + on-device pattern matching (7 languages). Images through PhotoDNA (CSAM) + Cloud Vision (explicit) + Hive Moderation (drugs, weapons, gore). URLs through Google Web Risk. Returns threat categories, confidence, behavioral signals, and actions.

Why This Model Works — The Research

Custorian's design isn't guesswork. Every decision — from showing parents patterns instead of messages, to giving children a help button instead of hiding the app — is grounded in decades of child psychology research.

Authoritative Parenting

Children with parents who monitor with warmth and explanation show better outcomes than those with restrictive or permissive parents. Custorian alerts inform — they don't spy.

Baumrind, 1991 — Developmental Psychology
Active Mediation > Restriction

Restrictive monitoring increases secrecy and reduces disclosure. Active mediation — talking about risks, building understanding — reduces online harm more effectively.

Livingstone & Helsper, 2008 — Journal of Communication
Self-Determination Theory

Children need autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Surveillance undermines all three. Custorian preserves autonomy (no message reading), builds competence (safety tips), and strengthens relatedness (conversation guides).

Deci & Ryan, 2000 — American Psychologist
Disclosure Over Detection

40% of children never tell anyone about online abuse. The #1 predictor of whether a child discloses is whether they believe they won't get in trouble. Custorian's child messaging explicitly says: "Nobody will get in trouble."

EU Kids Online, 2020 — LSE
Teens Respond to Education, Not Restriction

Adolescents who receive digital literacy education engage in fewer risky behaviors online than those who are simply restricted. Custorian's "Did you know?" facts and slang decoder are educational by design.

Yardi & Bruckman, 2011 — ACM CHI
APA Guidelines on Digital Media

The American Psychological Association recommends monitoring that is "developmentally appropriate, transparent, and preserves children's growing autonomy." Custorian's age-based profiles (5–18) follow this exactly.

APA, 2023 — Health Advisory on Social Media Use

Custorian is designed around a simple principle: children who feel protected — not watched — are more likely to ask for help when they need it.
