Get Involved

Help build the standard.

Custorian is developed in the open. Lend your expertise to the Advisory Board, or join the foreningen as a member. The standard belongs to everyone who helps shape it.

The Advisory Board advises; the elected Board governs. Membership is the route to standing for the Board.

Express interest in the Advisory Board.

The Advisory Board is 7–9 experts across child rights, law & regulation, technology & security, academic research, and law enforcement, plus a youth representative (16–21). It advises on the standard and ethical oversight — it does not govern. Members are invited by the foreningen; tell us where you would contribute.
