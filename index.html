Get involved in governance.

The Governance Board is Custorian’s primary governing body, holding ultimate fiduciary and safeguarding accountability across the association, without directing day-to-day operations. Seats are elected by the general assembly for renewable two-year terms, unpaid, with four to six Board meetings a year, held virtually. Directors are expected to bring a strong professional network and help open doors to funding and partnerships. You cannot be employed by, or hold a financial interest in, a platform subject to the standard, or have any employment, contractual, or financial relationship with any Custorian arm, its commercial technology company, or a recognised Certification Body within the last 36 months.

The Advisory Board is a roster of ten to twelve named experts who provide ad hoc, per-task input on the standard, its ethical dimensions, and child-facing content, drawn from child rights, legal, technology & security, academic, clinical, law enforcement, and youth backgrounds. There is no fixed meeting cadence: work happens as discrete review tasks, formalised through an individual MOU. It advises. It does not govern.

Become a member.

Foreningen Custorian is a Danish non-profit association (CVR 46399455) with two membership classes. Support Members are individuals and civil-society organisations who back the mission; support membership carries no governing vote. Governing Members hold the voting rights of the general assembly and confirm the Board; admission to this class is restricted to those meeting the criteria set out in the association's statutes. About Governing Membership. Governing Members hold the voting rights of Custorian's General Assembly (the association's highest authority), including the vote that confirms the Board each term. Per the Governance Charter (Article III), this class is restricted to persons meeting the criteria set out in the association's statutes; platforms, entities certified under the standard, and entities seeking certification are not eligible for either membership class. A statutes amendment formally establishing Governing Membership is still pending adoption at the next general assembly, so until it passes, the Board reviews each application on its own facts rather than against a fixed checklist.
