What flows up

Alert category, severity level, timestamp, age bracket. That's it. No messages. No names. No device IDs. No way to trace an alert to a child.

What stays on-device

All message content. All photos. All alert details. The parent dashboard. Everything that could identify a child stays on the phone, encrypted locally.

Who controls consent

Parents opt in to school mode when the school deploys Custorian. One tap to opt out at any time. Without opt-in, the family app works fully — just no data flows to the school.

3 validated languages

English, Danish, and German — independently validated for detection accuracy. We publish precision, recall, and false positive rates quarterly. We don't claim languages we haven't proven.
