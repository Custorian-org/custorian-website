The EU ePrivacy derogation expired April 3, 2026. The DSA, AI Act, AADC, and Online Safety Act all mandate child protection. None of them specify how. There is no shared, auditable standard for child digital safety. Custorian is building it.

The Standard

Comprehensive.
Auditable. Open.

The Custorian Controls Framework defines 137 auditable controls across six assessment domains. It is cross-mapped to the DSA, GDPR, AI Act, UK Age Appropriate Design Code, Online Safety Act, COPPA, and UNCRC. Published under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Certification

Custorian Certified.

Platforms that serve children can certify against the Controls Framework. Certification is voluntary, independent, and public. Like PCI DSS for payments. Like EPEAT for electronics.

01

Apply

The platform requests assessment against the Controls Framework.

02

Assess

Independent assessment using automated analysis, structured testing, and expert review.

03

Certify

Platforms that meet the standard are listed on the public Registry as Custorian Certified.

Certification is valid for 12 months with annual renewal. The Registry is public — parents, regulators, and procurement officers can verify which platforms are certified.
