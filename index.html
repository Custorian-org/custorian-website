Architecture

On-device by default. Intelligence by consent.

Message content never leaves the device. Anonymised metadata flows upstream only with explicit parental opt-in.

Child's device — AI runs locally
↓

↓

↓ aggregate
Municipal / national intelligence

What stays on-device

All message content. All photos. All alert details. Everything that could identify a child. Encrypted locally.

What flows up

Category, severity, timestamp, age bracket. No messages. No names. No device IDs. Untraceable to any individual.

3 validated languages

English, Danish, German. Independently validated. We publish accuracy metrics quarterly. We don't claim what we haven't proven.

Federated learning

Models improve across all devices without extracting data. Every user makes every other user safer.
