Compliance standards for child digital safety

The EU ePrivacy derogation expired April 3, 2026. DSA, AI Act, AADC, and Online Safety Act mandate child protection. None specify how. There is no shared, auditable standard. Custorian is building it.

The Standard

Comprehensive. Auditable. Open.

The Custorian Controls Framework defines 137 auditable controls across six assessment domains: age assurance, content and design safety, data privacy, parental oversight, threat detection and response, and governance. It is cross-mapped to the DSA, GDPR, AI Act, UK Age Appropriate Design Code, Online Safety Act, COPPA, and UNCRC. The framework is published under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Certification

Custorian Certified.

Platforms that serve children can certify against the Custorian Controls Framework. Certification is voluntary, independent, and public. Certified platforms are listed on the Custorian Registry — a public record of which platforms meet the standard. Like PCI DSS for payment security. Like EPEAT for sustainable electronics.

Apply
The platform requests assessment against the Controls Framework.
Assess
Independent assessment using automated analysis, structured testing, and expert review.
Certify
Platforms that meet the standard are listed on the public Registry as Custorian Certified.

Certification is valid for 12 months and requires annual renewal. The Registry is public — parents, regulators, and procurement officers can check which platforms are certified.

Current State

Pre-launch.

The Controls Framework v0.1 is published. The white paper is on Zenodo. The certification programme and assessment methodology are in development. No platform has been certified yet. Pilot certification begins in 2026 with consenting platforms. The Advisory Council is forming.
