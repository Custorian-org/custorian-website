What it is

Bornalog comes from børn, children, and analog: a day back in a world before screens. Music, dancing, races, crafts and a stamp passport, with no phone needed for any of it.

Bornalog Fest is organised by Foreningen Custorian, a Danish non-profit working to protect children online. All profits go to that work. In the parents’ corner, families can get practical help with online safety and join our free parents’ alliance.
