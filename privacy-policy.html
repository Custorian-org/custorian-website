Privacy, plainly.

What we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how we protect it. This applies to custorian.org and to Custorian's membership, donation and payment processes.

Foreningen Custorian CVR 46399455 Copenhagen, Denmark Version 1.0 · effective 7 September 2026

Who we are

Foreningen Custorian (CVR 46399455, Copenhagen, Denmark) is the data controller for personal data processed through custorian.org, its membership and donation forms, and its payment flows. Questions or requests go to info@custorian.org.

What we collect, and why

  • Membership and donation data: name, email, and (for organisation membership) organisation name, collected when you join, donate, or make an enquiry. Used to administer your membership or gift and to contact you about it.
  • Payment data: when you pay via Stripe (card) or MobilePay, your payment details are entered directly into that provider's own secure checkout, not into Custorian's systems. We receive confirmation of the payment, the amount, and the billing name/email, not your card or account number.
  • Enquiry and outreach data: name, email, organisation and any message you send us via a form (pilot applications, advisory interest, general enquiries) or by email, held to respond to you and, where relevant, pursue the association's non-profit purpose.
  • Website analytics: cookieless, first-party analytics (page views, referrers) via Custorian's own script. No third-party trackers and no advertising cookies are used, so no cookie consent banner is shown.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website. It is scoped to adults, organisations and professional contacts; it is not directed at children, and none of Custorian's own web forms are intended for use by a child.

Legal basis

We process membership and donation data under contract / legitimate interest (administering your membership or gift), enquiry data under legitimate interest (responding to you and pursuing our non-profit purpose), and payment confirmation data under contract (fulfilling the membership or donation you requested). Where Danish association law requires us to keep a member register or financial records, that processing rests on legal obligation.

Who we share it with, and how

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for their own marketing. Data passes to the following processors, each strictly to deliver the service you requested:

Where a processor is based outside the EU/EEA, that transfer relies on the processor's own GDPR-compliant safeguards (standard contractual clauses or equivalent). Custorian does not itself transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA on its own initiative.

Retention

Member data is kept for as long as you are a member, then deleted, subject to Danish bookkeeping law requiring certain financial records to be kept for five years. Donation records are kept as long as required for accounting purposes. Enquiry and outreach data is kept only as long as relevant to the purpose it was collected for, and is reviewed periodically.

Security

Access to membership and stakeholder data is restricted to Custorian's working team through a login-gated internal system with row-level access control and no public write access. Payment card data is handled entirely by Stripe and MobilePay under their own PCI-compliant security standards; Custorian's systems never receive or store it. Financial and banking credentials are held by the chairperson only.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or provide a copy of your personal data, or object to how we use it, by writing to info@custorian.org. We respond within one month. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page as our data practices change and note the effective date above. Material changes affecting members or donors will be communicated by email.

Status. This policy reflects Custorian's actual current data practices as of the effective date above. It is published for transparency and to meet payment-provider requirements; a fuller internal Data Protection & GDPR Compliance Policy is being finalised with external legal counsel and will be reflected here as it is adopted.
