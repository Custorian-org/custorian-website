Child Safeguarding Policy

Safeguarding is not incidental to Custorian’s work, it is the reason the association exists.

Foreningen Custorian CVR 46399455 Version 1.0 · adopted 5 July 2026 Next review: 5 July 2028

Owner: Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL), the Chair, pending appointment of a dedicated Child Safety Officer.

1. Purpose and commitment

Foreningen Custorian exists to protect children and young people from digital threats. Safeguarding is therefore not incidental to our work, it is our reason for existing, and we hold ourselves to it.

Custorian is committed to creating and maintaining a safe environment for every child and young person who comes into contact with our work, whether through youth consultation, research, participation panels, events, or online channels. We have zero tolerance of any form of abuse, exploitation, or harm to children, and of any conduct by a person associated with Custorian that places a child at risk.

The best interests of the child are the primary consideration in everything we do, in line with the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (including General Comment No. 25 on children’s rights in the digital environment).

2. Scope, who and what this covers

This policy applies to everyone acting on behalf of Custorian, the board, employees, contractors, consultants, volunteers, researchers, and partners, in any activity that involves, reaches, or affects children and young people (under 18). It applies to in-person and online activity alike.

3. Definitions

  • Child / young person: any person under 18.
  • Safeguarding: the responsibility to prevent harm to children and to respond appropriately where harm is suspected or disclosed.
  • Harm: physical, sexual, emotional abuse, neglect, exploitation (including online), or exposure to such.
  • Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL): the named person responsible for safeguarding decisions and for receiving and acting on concerns. At present this is the Chair; Custorian will appoint a dedicated Child Safety Officer (CSO) as it grows, consistent with control CS-GO.1.1 of the Custorian standard.

4. Safeguarding across our work

4.1 People

  • Safe recruitment: anyone in a role with direct or repeated contact with children shall be recruited with appropriate checks, including a criminal-record / child-protection check (e.g. Danish børneattest) where the role warrants it, references, and a clear role description.
  • Code of Conduct: everyone acting for Custorian shall follow the Code of Conduct at Annex A, including never being alone and unobserved with a child without justification, never engaging in private/unsanctioned contact with a child met through our work, and always behaving in a manner that is professional, respectful, and age-appropriate.
  • Training and awareness: those involved in activities with children shall receive safeguarding briefing appropriate to their role, and shall know how to raise a concern.

4.2 Activities and programmes

  • Risk assessment: a proportionate safeguarding risk assessment shall be completed before any activity that involves children directly (e.g. a youth-consultation session, participation panel, or event), and mitigations put in place.
  • Informed consent: the informed consent of the child and, where required by age, of a parent or guardian shall be obtained before a child participates, before any personal data is collected, and before any image, quote, or contribution is used. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.
  • Child participation: children’s participation shall be voluntary, age-appropriate, safe, and non-tokenistic, and shall never expose a child to material or questions that could cause distress without appropriate support in place.

4.3 Data and communication

  • Children’s personal data shall be collected only where necessary, minimised, protected, and retained only as long as needed, consistent with the GDPR (including Article 8) and with Custorian’s own standard (data-minimisation and on-device principles, domain CS-DM).
  • Communication about or with children shall protect their privacy and dignity; identifying details shall not be published without consent.

5. Reporting and responding to concerns

Anyone who has a safeguarding concern, whether a suspicion, a disclosure by a child, or a breach of this policy or the Code of Conduct, shall report it without delay to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (info@custorian.org).

Steps:

  • Ensure immediate safety. If a child is in immediate danger, contact emergency services (112 in Denmark) first.
  • Report the concern to the DSL as soon as possible, with the facts as known. Do not investigate or interview the child yourself.
  • The DSL records the concern, assesses risk, and, where there is actual or suspected harm, refers to the competent authorities (Danish municipal child services / den sociale døgnvagt, police, and, for online child sexual abuse material, the competent reporting channel) in line with legal duties.
  • Confidentiality is maintained; information is shared only on a need-to-know basis and as required by law. There will be no retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Children and young people shall be told, in age-appropriate terms, how to raise a concern and that they will be listened to.

6. Governance and review

The board holds overall responsibility for safeguarding. This policy shall be reviewed at least every two years, or sooner following any incident or material change in activity. Adoption and review are recorded in the board minutes.

Annex A — Code of Conduct (summary)

Everyone acting for Custorian shall:

  • Treat all children with respect and recognise their rights, dignity, and evolving capacities;
  • Never engage in, condone, or ignore any form of abuse, exploitation, or harm;
  • Never establish private or unsupervised contact (including online/social media) with a child met through Custorian’s work;
  • Never use language or behaviour that is inappropriate, harassing, discriminatory, or sexually suggestive toward a child;
  • Respect boundaries and consent, and avoid being alone and unobserved with a child without justification;
  • Report any concern, disclosure, or breach immediately to the Designated Safeguarding Lead.
Breach. Breach of this Code may result in removal from the activity, termination of engagement, and referral to authorities where a child may be at risk.
