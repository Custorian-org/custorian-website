How Custorian is governed

The board

Foreningen Custorian is governed by a Board of Directors elected by the general assembly. Under the articles (§ 5, v1.2), the board is one to five members serving staggered two-year terms. The founder holds a seat by right and chairs the board until 31 December 2031 — a time-boxed continuity arrangement, published openly here; thereafter the board elects its chair. Protected provisions (§ 10) prevent any majority from changing the association’s purpose, selling or exclusively licensing the standard, or admitting commercial members, and the standard’s technical content sits outside the general assembly’s vote (§ 5.6). Commercial companies — including any platform that is or may become subject to assessment under the standard — are not eligible for membership (§ 3.1) and hold no board seats; as the board grows, seats are drawn from academic researchers, civil-society organisations, hotline operators, independent child-safety experts, youth representation, and an independent chair.

The association does not certify platforms

Certification is issued by independent, accredited Certification Bodies operating under a published scheme. The association maintains the scheme and licenses the “Custorian Certified” trademark to qualifying bodies. It does not audit or certify platforms itself. This separation is deliberate: the body that writes the rules is not the body that grades the exam.

The ten firewalls

A standard is only independent if it cannot be captured. Firewalls keep the standard independent and prevent capture, including: no commercial seats on the board (commercial companies are not eligible for membership, § 3.1); identical certification terms for all applicants; a 30% cap on any single funder; executive-overlap limited to 24 months; a biennial independent governance review; a whistleblower channel to an external ombudsperson; and a revocable trademark licence. Every board decision is published with its rationale. There is no pay-to-play. Should any commercial entity ever be involved in implementation, it is bound on identical terms and holds no vote — the independence of the standard does not depend on who builds the tools.

Why this page exists. Custorian asks platforms to prove their claims about children rather than assert them. We hold ourselves to the same test. Governance you cannot inspect is a marketing claim; governance you can inspect is a commitment.

Decisions & minutes

The board publishes a summary of every material decision, with the reasoning behind it. We publish approved decisions and their rationale rather than verbatim minutes — this keeps individuals, third parties and unfinished deliberations out of the public record while keeping the association’s choices fully accountable.
