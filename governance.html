How Custorian is governed

The board

Foreningen Custorian is governed by a Board of Directors. Board seats are filled by resolution of the sitting board and presented to the general assembly for ratification, not by direct membership election. Under the articles (§ 5), the board serves two-year terms and includes a Chair, Vice Chair, Treasurer, Secretary and one or more Non-Executive Directors. The founder holds a seat by right and chairs the board until 31 December 2031 (a time-boxed continuity arrangement, published openly here); thereafter the board elects its chair. Protected provisions (§ 10) prevent any majority from changing the association’s purpose, selling or exclusively licensing the standard, or admitting commercial members without the founder’s written consent, and the standard’s technical content sits outside the general assembly’s vote (§ 5.6). Commercial companies, including any platform that is or may become subject to assessment under the standard, are not eligible for membership (§ 3.1) and hold no board seats. The board oversees the association’s operating arms (below) and recuses from the day-to-day decisions of the advisory bodies and the Certification & Impartiality Committee.

Advisory bodies

A single, non-governing Advisory Board of ten to twelve named individual experts, spanning child rights, legal and regulatory, technology, academic and clinical practice, law enforcement, and youth perspectives, feeds independent, ad hoc, non-binding input into the standard, its ethical dimensions, and child-facing content; it holds no vote and cannot bind the board. Members are drawn from that one roster into focused sub-committees as needed: Standards & Technical (technical evolution and peer review of the standard), Certification & Impartiality (protects the certification firewall and arbitrates appeals, with its own independence safeguards, including the founder’s recusal from membership), and Policy & Rights (regulatory, academic and societal grounding).

Four arms, kept apart on purpose

Operations sit in four arms, each with its own scope and funding source, so that no single arm can quietly capture the others. An Association arm handles membership, fundraising and donor stewardship, with a strict firewall against platform funding. A Standards arm owns the standard and control framework, independent of any commercial funding. A Certification arm manages the certification scheme and professional credentialing, and never audits a platform directly. And a Research arm runs studies and corpus validation, funded solely by grants.

The association does not certify platforms

Platform certification is issued exclusively by independent bodies accredited under ISO/IEC 17065, operating under a published scheme; the association maintains the scheme and licenses the “Custorian Certified” trademark to qualifying bodies, and never audits or certifies a platform itself. A separate, in-house professional credentialing track (for auditors and child safety officers) runs under ISO/IEC 17024 through the Certification arm. Credentialing people is not the same activity as certifying platforms, and neither is confused with the other.

The firewalls

A standard is only independent if it cannot be captured. Firewalls keep it that way: only external, accredited bodies decide platform certification; the Research arm holds no monetary ties to any platform it studies; the association takes no funding, in any form, from a platform or company that is or may become subject to assessment under its own standard, or from anyone that controls or substantially funds one; the founder recuses from any decision touching their own employer; the association itself has no shares to sell; the Data Protection Officer reports independently, outside the revenue-generating arms; no one from the Certification arm sits on the Impartiality Committee; and professional credentialing is preferred for certification bodies, never a mandatory gate. Every board decision is published with its rationale. There is no pay-to-play.

Why this page exists. Custorian asks platforms to prove their claims about children rather than assert them. We hold ourselves to the same test. Governance you cannot inspect is a marketing claim; governance you can inspect is a commitment.

Membership & support

Foreningen Custorian has a single membership class, Support Membership, across the four tiers below. Support Members hold no vote on the composition of the Board: Board seats are filled by resolution of the sitting Board and presented to the general assembly for ratification. Support Members retain full voting rights on all other matters reserved to the general assembly, including the right to attend, propose agenda items, and vote on budget and accounts. No fee tier buys influence over the standard: membership never confers, and cannot be exchanged for, certification, and a for-profit member’s own platform or product stays subject to the same independent, accredited assessment as everyone else’s.

Fees were set by the Board on 21 August 2026 (organisation fee revised 8 September 2026; trust & safety expert and for-profit tiers added 9 September 2026) and are pending formal ratification at the next general assembly.

Cancelling a membership subscription. Dues are billed annually and can be cancelled at any time before the next renewal, with no cancellation fee, through the Stripe Customer Portal: the “Manage subscription” link in your payment receipt and renewal emails lets you cancel yourself, at any time, no need to contact us. You can also cancel by writing to info@custorian.org with the name and email used at payment (we confirm within two business days). Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the period already paid for, and membership, including any voting rights attached to it, ends when that paid period expires.

Donations

Custorian is permitted by Indsamlingsnævnet (the Danish Fundraising Board) under §3 of Indsamlingsloven to collect public donations from 12 August 2026 to 11 August 2027, for project-specific expenses that advance children’s safety online, and operating costs. This channel is for individual donations only. Separately, and regardless of channel, Custorian never accepts donations, grants or sponsorship from a platform or company that is or may become subject to assessment under the standard, or from anyone that controls or substantially funds one, in line with the Funding Policy below. Giving stays separate from influence, the same firewall that shapes membership.

Decisions & minutes

The board publishes a summary of every material decision, with the reasoning behind it. We publish approved decisions and their rationale rather than verbatim minutes. This keeps individuals, third parties and unfinished deliberations out of the public record while keeping the association’s choices fully accountable.
