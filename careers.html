Careers at Custorian.

We are a small team building a European standard for children’s safety online, the research behind it, and the infrastructure that helps take child sexual abuse material down faster. These are the roles we are looking to fill now.

02

Advisory Board: open seats

Our Advisory Board gives independent, non-binding advice to the Board and the standard’s committees. Seats are unpaid and non-voting, formalised by a memorandum of understanding, and take a few meetings a year plus written advice when a question comes up. These five are open now; read more in our governance section.

Legal and regulatory Knowledge of the Digital Services Act, the GDPR, the Audiovisual Media Services Directive, national online safety law or children’s rights law. You would advise on the legal references in the standard and on how it lines up with regulation. Apply for this seat →
Technology and security Experience in security engineering, trust and safety, privacy-enhancing technology or age assurance. You would help peer review the standard’s technical controls and the tools we build. Apply for this seat →
Law enforcement Current or former experience of child exploitation investigations, victim identification or reporting of illegal content. You would advise on how reports and evidence flow between hotlines, providers and police. Apply for this seat →
Youth perspectives Experience of bringing children’s and young people’s views into decisions, for example through a youth council or youth participation work. You would help make sure the standard reflects what young people actually experience online. Apply for this seat →
Ethics Background in applied, research or technology ethics, or children’s rights, ideally with ethics committee experience. You would advise on consent and assent in our research, the balance between safety and children’s privacy and autonomy, and the ethics of data use and AI in our work. Apply for this seat →

Applying opens our Advisory Board form with the seat selected. While in post you cannot hold a paid or advisory role with an online service the standard covers.

03

How we work

  • Remote first. We work across Europe, with a weekly check-in and written decisions.
  • Independence. While in post you cannot hold a paid or advisory role with an online service the standard covers, or with a company selling products or services related to it.
  • Safeguarding. Roles that touch work with or about children need a børneattest or the equivalent check from your country, and a volunteer agreement covering confidentiality, data protection and safeguarding.
  • Expenses. Pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursed.

We welcome applications from everyone, whatever their background, and we assess every application on the skills and experience it shows. If you meet most of what a role asks but not all, we would still like to hear from you.
