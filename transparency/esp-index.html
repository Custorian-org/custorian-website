01

What an ESP is

An electronic service provider stores the file. A registrar controls the domain name that points to it. They are different companies, answerable for different things.

When abuse material is reported, the removal notice usually goes to the host. Hotlines and researchers publish figures about hosts: how many notices, how long removal took, how often the material came back. They do not publish the same figures about registrars. This index brings the host figures together, and the Intermediary Accountability Index covers the registrar layers.

04

Method and limits

  • Published sources only. Every figure comes from a report by the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (Project Arachnid) or the CSAM Hosting Monitor run by TU Delft with EOKM. We add nothing to what they published.
  • A record of what was published, not a current finding. Each row is dated to its source period. The newest figures are from the year shown against them, and a provider may have changed since.
  • Hosts, not registrars. The sources name hosting providers and do not name registrars. Registrar-level response times are not published by anyone; that gap is what the registrar layers of the index exist to close.
05

Right of reply

If you represent a provider listed here and a figure is wrong, out of date or missing context, write to info@custorian.org. We acknowledge within 5 working days and respond within 20, and we correct the record where the source supports it.
