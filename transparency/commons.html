04

Who we never take money from

The Funding Policy is an absolute bar. It has no minimum amount and no percentage below which it stops applying.

  • Any platform or service that is, or may become, subject to assessment under the standard. Eligibility for assessment is the test, not whether it is being assessed today.
  • Any company that controls, or is controlled by, such a platform, and anyone that substantially funds one.
  • Donations, grants and sponsorship alike. The bar covers every form of giving, whatever it is called.

The same firewall shapes membership: platforms subject to assessment and their affiliates cannot join, and hold no board seats (Articles § 3.1). No payment of any kind buys a say over the content of the standard, which sits outside the general assembly’s vote entirely (§ 5.8).

Public donations. Custorian is permitted by Indsamlingsnævnet (the Danish Fundraising Board) under §3 of Indsamlingsloven to collect public donations from 12 August 2026 to 11 August 2027, for project-specific expenses that advance children’s safety online and for operating costs. That channel is for individual donations only.
