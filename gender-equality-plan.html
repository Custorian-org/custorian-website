Public document. Published at custorian.org/gender-equality-plan and reviewed annually.

Applicability note. The Horizon Europe Gender Equality Plan eligibility criterion is mandatory for public bodies, higher-education institutions and research organisations. NGOs are not within the mandate, but a non-profit that performs research may be validated as a research organisation, in which case the criterion applies. Custorian adopts this plan as good practice and as a precaution, and applies it to CERV, Horizon Europe and all other funded work.

1. Purpose and commitment

Custorian commits to gender equality in its own organisation and in the work it produces. This plan sets out the structural measures that support that commitment, following the four mandatory building blocks and five recommended thematic areas of the Horizon Europe Gender Equality Plan criterion. It is sized for a founder-led, volunteer-run association and scales with the organisation under §8.0.

2. Scope

Applies to the Board, Advisory Board, Research Ethics Oversight reviewers, staff, contractors, volunteers and the commercial subsidiary, and to all research, standard-setting, product and communications work carried out in Custorian's name.

3. Mandatory building blocks

3.1 Public document

This plan is a formal document, published on custorian.org, signed by the Chair of the Board and adopted by Board resolution recorded in the minutes. It is referenced in the induction pack under Governance Charter §10.2 and in the policies compilation.

3.2 Dedicated resources

The Chair is the named owner of this plan until a Gender Equality Lead is designated by the Board. Time for implementation and monitoring is allocated within the annual budget approved under statutes §5.12, and expenses arising under it (training, data collection tooling, external advice) are reimbursable under Policy 4. Where a funded project carries a gender-equality work package or budget line, that budget is ring-fenced for the purposes in this plan.

3.3 Data collection and monitoring

Custorian collects sex-disaggregated data on: Board, Advisory Board and sub-committee composition; staff, contractor and volunteer roles; applicants and appointees for every role advertised; participants in youth consultation, research and events; and authorship and speaking roles in Custorian's public outputs. Data is collected at appointment or participation, held at the Internal tier under Policy 9, and reported annually to the Board with the risk register. Where numbers are small enough to identify an individual, figures are reported as ranges.

3.4 Training and awareness

Every person joining Custorian in a governance, staff or volunteer role receives this plan with the induction pack and confirms in writing that they have read it. The Board receives a short annual briefing on gender equality and unconscious bias, delivered by the plan owner or an external provider, minuted as part of the annual board effectiveness review under Governance Charter §4.9b. Safeguarding training under Policy 1 includes gender-based violence and harassment.

4. Thematic areas

4.1 Work-life balance and organisational culture

Custorian operates remotely and asynchronously by default. Meetings are scheduled inside ordinary working hours across participants' time zones, with materials circulated in advance so that contribution does not depend on live attendance. Board service is unpaid and designed around four to six virtual meetings a year. Custorian does not expect availability outside agreed hours and does not treat responsiveness outside those hours as a measure of commitment. Parental, caring and health-related absence is accommodated without penalty to role or standing.

4.2 Gender balance in leadership and decision-making

The Board, Advisory Board and each sub-committee aim for a composition in which no gender exceeds two thirds of members. Where a body falls outside that range, the Board records the fact and the steps being taken in the annual report under §3.3. Recruitment for the Governance Board under Charter §10.2 uses open calls alongside targeted outreach, and shortlists for every seat are reviewed for gender balance before appointment.

4.3 Gender equality in recruitment and career progression

Role descriptions state responsibilities and required competencies, not personal characteristics, and are checked for gendered language before publication. Selection is against the published criteria and recorded. Where two or more people interview a candidate, the panel is mixed where practicable. Volunteers and contractors are offered the same access to training, authorship and speaking opportunities regardless of gender, and the plan owner reviews the distribution of those opportunities annually.

4.4 Integration of the gender dimension in research and standards content

Custorian's research, Watchtower and Verify assessments, Registrar Watch analysis, classifier evaluation and the CDSW Standard itself consider whether risks, harms and protective measures differ by sex and gender, and by intersecting factors such as age, disability, migration background and care setting. Data collected in research is sex-disaggregated by default and reported that way unless doing so would identify an individual. Classifier and detection work reports performance by language and, where the data permits, by the gender of the simulated or consenting participant, under Policy 10 §1. Standard controls that address grooming, sextortion, cyberbullying and self-harm are drafted with attention to how these harms are gendered in the evidence base, and consultation under Policy 13 §5.0 invites comment on that dimension. Findings on gender-differentiated harm are published with the same sourcing standard as any other Custorian output under Policy 6 §11.0.

4.5 Measures against gender-based violence, including sexual harassment

Custorian has zero tolerance of gender-based violence, sexual harassment and discrimination in any Custorian setting, in person or online. Any concern is reported under Policy 11 (Whistleblowing and Protected Disclosure), with the same protection from retaliation and the same recusal rule where a Board member is implicated. Concerns involving a child are additionally handled under Policy 1. Confirmed conduct is grounds for removal or termination under Policy 3 §6.0 and Governance Charter §4.5. Custorian's Code of Conduct (Policy 1, Annex A) applies to conduct toward adults as well as children in respect of harassment and discriminatory behaviour.

6. Relationship to other policies

Conduct toward children: Policy 1. Reporting and protection: Policy 11. Conflicts and sanctions: Policy 3. Data collected under this plan: Policy 9 (Internal tier) and Policy 5. Gender dimension in AI systems: Policy 10. Gender dimension in the Standard: Policy 13 consultation process.

7. Reporting

The plan owner reports to the Board annually, alongside the risk register review under Governance Charter §15.1, on the indicators in §5.0, progress against targets and any changes proposed. A summary is published with the annual report.

8. Scaling

When Custorian has five or more paid staff, or coordinates a funded project with three or more partners, the Board designates a Gender Equality Lead distinct from the Chair, adopts a written recruitment procedure with mixed panels as a requirement rather than an aim, and commissions an external review of this plan.

9. Review

Reviewed annually, on any material change in organisational size or structure, and before submission of any application that relies on it.
