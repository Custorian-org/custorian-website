Draft Standard

One framework, every source of law

The Child Digital Safety and Wellbeing (CDSW) Standard converts the DSA and its Article 28 Guidelines, the GDPR, the AI Act, the AVMSD, the proposed CSA Regulation, the Lanzarote and Budapest Conventions, the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child and General Comment No. 25, and the UK, Australian and US regimes into a single testable control framework: 160+ controls across 10 domains, applied through an applicability matrix covering 11 platform types, from social media and messaging to AI companions, app stores and immersive environments. Every control is traced to its source authority.

The standard is in progress with industry peers through our Standards Advisory Committee. Once finalized, it will be published here in full. For the current state of the framework, or to ask a question, get in touch.
